The Empowered Strategist × “Assumptions replace real audience understanding” · Psychological angle
The signal in the data

Across 84 posts, brands broadcast product features to “Entrepreneurs” as a single block — yet SOMIN's own audience detection splits them into 4 distinct personas with opposing needs. The category talks at a demographic that does not behave as one.

Why it works

People act on being understood, not being targeted. Naming the buyer's real tension — “I'm guessing who my audience is” — earns permission before a single feature is mentioned.

The insight
Understanding is the foundation of connection — the brand that maps the person, not the segment, wins the relationship before the sale.
